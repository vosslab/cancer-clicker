# Related projects

Cancer Clicker gives browser-game players an active-and-idle incremental loop: harvest resources,
buy mutations, and watch a single cell become an endless visual colony.

## Possible related projects

### Cookie Clicker

- Relationship: Direct alternative or competitor.
- Link: [Cookie Clicker](https://www.cookieclicker.com/)
- Why visitors may care: It offers the same click, buy, and accumulate loop for players who want a
  long-running browser idle game.
- Evidence: The official site calls Cookie Clicker the original idle game and links its free web
  version.
- Confidence: Likely.

### Cell to Singularity

- Relationship: Same-workflow project or independent implementation.
- Link: [Cell to Singularity](https://celltosingularity.com/)
- Why visitors may care: It lets players begin with a single cell, tap for points, and buy upgrades
  while exploring a biology-themed progression.
- Evidence: Its official site describes an idle game that starts with a single-cell organism and
  uses clicker gameplay to unlock biological milestones.
- Confidence: Likely.

### EvoCell BioClicker

- Relationship: Same-workflow project or independent implementation.
- Link: [EvoCell BioClicker](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3574920/EvoCell_BioClicker/)
- Why visitors may care: It provides another cell-focused incremental colony experience for players
  interested in evolving organisms through idle progression.
- Evidence: The developer's Steam page describes an organic cell automaton simulator with an
  incremental idle goal of colonizing and evolving a species.
- Confidence: Possible.

### Universal Paperclips

- Relationship: Prior art or inspiration.
- Link: [Universal Paperclips](https://www.decisionproblem.com/paperclips/index2.html)
- Why visitors may care: It offers a compact idle-management progression to visitors who want to
  compare how a simple production loop can expand into a larger system.
- Evidence: New York University Game Center describes Frank Lantz's game as an idle, management,
  strategy simulation about building a paperclip empire and links its browser version.
- Confidence: Likely.

## Evidence notes

Two focused discovery rounds began with the repository's visual, endless clicker loop and widened
to biology-themed incremental games and established idle-game examples. Each entry passes the
visitor relevance gate because it gives players a comparable clicker loop, a cell or evolution
theme, or a useful prior-art comparison. The links point to official game sites, a developer's
store page, or the publisher's university page; this guide does not imply endorsement or a shared
codebase.
