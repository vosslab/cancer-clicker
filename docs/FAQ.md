# Cancer Clicker FAQ

Answers for players who want to understand the endless clicker loop before they start a colony.

## What kind of game is this?

Cancer Clicker is a fictional, visual incremental game. Click a transformed cell to gain resources,
buy mutations that improve future gains, and build an ever messier colony. It is a stylized strategy
toy, not medical guidance or a model of cancer biology or treatment outcomes.

## How do I start growing?

- Select **Awaken cell** to begin the live simulation.
- Click the founding cell to harvest nutrients, energy, and biomass immediately.
- Spend the named resources on an affordable Mutation Shop recipe.
- Keep clicking while passive production steadily increases the resource stocks.

## Why are there three resources?

Each Mutation Shop recipe has a distinct cost, so the resource cards create a small planning loop.
Transporter swarm costs biomass, Glycolysis burst costs nutrients, Angiogenesis signal costs energy,
and Immune cloak costs both energy and biomass. Every purchase also changes the visible surface
signature on the founding cell.

## What do the `+amount/s` labels mean?

They show the actual passive change to each resource stock per second, rather than a decorative
estimate. Purchased mutations raise production, while bounded metabolic upkeep leaves every stock
with a positive passive surplus. Passive rates and resource stocks never become negative; a
resource is spent only by an affordable mutation purchase.

## Does the game pause or end?

No. Once awakened, the colony keeps accumulating while the page remains open. There is no victory
screen or reset at 100% host control. Instead, the game enters its endless lineage phase, where
daughter-cell layers continue to accumulate around the founding cell.

## Can I use the controls without a mouse?

Yes. Awaken, Restart, the founding cell, and every mutation are native buttons, so they can be
reached with the keyboard and show a visible focus state.
